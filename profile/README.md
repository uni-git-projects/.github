# University Projects

Multiple uni projects in software engineering, data science, embedded systems, & other advanced computer science.

### University Statistics - Personal

Overall, my average project performance is distinction, with an average personal contribution rate of ~80-90% per collaborative `.git` project. This doesn't account for the total number of collaborators, aside from the minimum of 2 per project. Collaboration does not imply contribution, & of over 300 collaborators across over 100 projects, probably barely more than half contributed in total. Thus, some `.git` projects may only have one contributor, but are still collaborative in nature, at least in the sense of credit. 

I write this retrospectively on a spur not to gloat, which I assume the statistics prove contrary to, but, furthermore, because of ineffably cyclic random bias conspiring & unnatural oppression every step of the way, globally, as if the complete opposite in every possible aspect; as if just instead. What I can gloat about, although likely now post-"SI" redundant, is achieving this while recovering (& suffering) from multiple, consecutive severe injuries, under eternal 24/7 wrongdoing plagues & corresponding misrepresentation; probably one, if not the most epic in each case, at least in context, in at least modern history of developed society, & 100% independently without any support; probably just as epic.
